Questions

Where do I pick up?

Porch pickup in Cedar Park, TX. We send the exact address when we confirm your order.

How fresh are the cookies?

Baked in small batches just before pickup day. They keep well for about a week in an airtight container, and the date rolls freeze nicely.

Allergies?

The cookies contain wheat, butter and eggs, and the date and chocolate rolls may contain nuts. Everything is made in a home kitchen where these ingredients are present. Add a note to your order or ask us before ordering.

Can I order for a party or holiday?

Yes. Add the date and quantity in the notes, or send a message and we'll work something out.

How do I pay?

Cash, Venmo or Zelle at pickup. There's no online payment on this site.
